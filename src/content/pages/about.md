Maël De Luca (Maël Abstract) is a Montréal-based visual artist exploring the beauty, complexity, and interconnectedness of the natural world. His practice is inspired by nature, mathematics, psychedelia, and the patterns he observes in the world around him, from branching rivers and roots to the structures of stars and the networks within the human body. Through vibrant colour, organic forms, geometric structures, and abstraction, he seeks to reveal the extraordinary within the familiar.

Maël began painting during a period of depression, when creating art became an unexpected way of accessing beauty that he could not otherwise feel. What began as a therapeutic process grew into a deeper fascination with perception and the mystery of the world. His work is an attempt to translate something that he feels cannot truly be expressed through words: a sense of interconnectedness, order, and beauty underlying the apparent chaos of reality.

His practice moves between contrasting processes. In his circle series, carefully established rules and thousands of repetitive gestures create meditative, intricate compositions in which simple forms develop into complex fractal-like patterns. In his more expressive psychedelic paintings, he works intuitively and spontaneously, allowing colour, movement, and instinct to guide the image as it emerges. Across both approaches, he is interested in the meeting point between order and chaos.

Colour is central to Maël's work. He sees it as a vital and joyful force and is drawn to bringing more of it into everyday life, including through his painted cars and other unconventional projects. Ultimately, his work is an invitation to look more closely at the world and to reconnect with a sense of childlike wonder, to become excited again by the complexity, beauty, and mystery of simply being alive.
